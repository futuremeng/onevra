---
title: Treemap
description: Nested sizes as tiles, such as disk usage, drilled into by clicking.
---

<Example name="treemap" height={420} />

Each node is a tile sized by its value, or by the sum of its children. Clicking a tile drills into it, and the breadcrumbs lead back; data changes morph the tiles. The arrow keys move between tiles, Enter drills in and Escape goes up.
