title: Chat

<Example name="chat" height={520} />

Send a message to see a turn run: an activity group shows the work, the reply streams in, and once the turn completes its steps fold behind a summary. The application owns the turns, their steps with stable IDs, the request and every label; builders never call the network.

`chat.FloatingConversation` lets the transcript scroll behind `chat.GlassComposer`, keeping the latest turn clear of it. Enter sends and Shift+Enter inserts a line. Stream replies with MyGo's `markdown.Stream`, and switch to `markdown.View` once complete.

The composer's slots hold attachments, pickers, a context meter and a footer; the package supplies chips, pickers, meters and usage limits styled for them. The [Gallery](/docs/starter-apps#gallery) shows a complete composer.
