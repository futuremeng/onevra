<Example name="floating-button-group" height={160} />

Use a floating button group for actions over content, such as a canvas or a text selection. Items are buttons or separators; `Clicked()` returns the ID of the activated button. The tray is one Tab stop, and the arrow keys move between its buttons.

`FloatingButtonGroupMuted` sits quietly on a page; `FloatingButtonGroupRaised` floats with a shadow. Pass `ui.Vertical` for a column of tools along a canvas edge.
