`components.Button` takes a label and a variant, and reports a click with `Clicked()` in the frame it happens. Use one primary button for the main action of a view, and quieter variants for the rest.

## With icons

Pass an empty label to give a button its own content, and keep its accessible name with `Label`.

<Example name="button-icons" height={140} />

## Icon buttons

`components.IconButton` shows only an icon, with its label as a tooltip. `IconButtonPlain` suits rows and headers; `IconButtonToolbar` raises the face to match `ButtonToolbar` and segmented tabs.

<Example name="icon-button" height={180} />

## Toggle buttons

`components.ToggleButton` turns a `*bool` on and off, filled with the accent while on, for formatting and view options.

<Example name="toggle-button" height={140} />

## Menus

`Menu` opens a menu when the button is clicked; `ContextMenu` opens one on right-click. Ask each item whether it was `Chosen()`.

<Example name="button-menu" height={180} />
