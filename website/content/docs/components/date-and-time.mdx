`CalendarPicker` shows a date and opens a calendar in a popover; `TimePicker` shows a 24-hour time and opens hour and minute columns. Choosing a date keeps the time of day, and choosing a time keeps the date, so both can edit the same value.

## Calendar

`components.Calendar` is the six-week view on its own. Click its title to switch to months, then years. The first day of the week follows the user's regional formats.
