<Example name="settings" height={460} />

Describe settings as categories of groups of items, each with a stable ID, a title and a builder for its control. Settings provides the navigation, search and row layout; the application keeps the values, persists them and decides when they take effect. Try searching for "audio" or "font": search matches titles, descriptions and keywords without building hidden controls.

Place Settings in a dialog, a shell or its own window; it fills a bounded parent. `Narrow` stacks rows and replaces the sidebar with a selector; choose it from the space Settings is given. `Stacked` places a wide control under its text, and `Content` builds a whole row.
