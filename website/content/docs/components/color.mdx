`components.ColorPicker` is a swatch that opens the editor in a popover. Change its shape with `Size`, `Radius`, `Border` and `Padding`.

## Editor

`components.ColorView` edits a color in place: a saturation and brightness plane, hue and opacity sliders, and RGB, HSB or HSL fields with a hex field. Its menu copies the color as text.
