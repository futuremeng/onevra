---
title: Text
description: Text in the theme's style, wrapping, truncated or editable in place.
---

<Example name="text" height={260} />

`components.Text` inherits the theme's font, size and color. With `TextOptions.Editable`, it edits a `*string` in place, like an inline name, without a field around it; `Changed()` and `Submitted()` report edits and Enter.
