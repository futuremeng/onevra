---
title: Slider
description: Picks a value, or a range, by dragging a thumb along a track.
---

<Example name="slider" height={240} />

A slider is continuous by default. `Step` snaps it and shows ticks; `Range` adds a second thumb, with the slider's value as the minimum. Tab moves between the thumbs and the arrow keys adjust the focused one.
