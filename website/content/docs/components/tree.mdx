---
title: Tree
description: Nested rows that expand and collapse, built directly or virtualized.
---

<Example name="tree" height={260} />

`components.Tree` builds `TreeItem` rows; a row with an `open` binding and children is a branch. Clicking the arrow or double-clicking the row toggles it, and Left and Right move between parents and children. The application owns expansion and selection.

## Large trees

`components.TreeVirtualized` builds only the visible rows from roots and a `children` function. Expansion is keyed, and Option or Shift with Left and Right expands recursively.
