---
title: Carousel
description: Slides browsed by dragging, swiping or arrows, with optional rotation.
---

<Example name="carousel" height={320} />

The active slide sits in the center, and its neighbors shrink and dim as a hint that there is more. Drag or flick the track, click a neighbor, or use the arrow keys. With an `Interval`, a play control rotates the slides, pausing on hover, focus and dragging.
