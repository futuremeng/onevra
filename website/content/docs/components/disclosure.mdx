---
title: Accordion and Collapsible
description: Sections that expand and collapse, in a bordered group or on their own.
---

<Example name="accordion" height={280} />

`components.Accordion` builds independently expandable sections in a bordered box. Each item binds its own `*bool`, so any number can be open; to keep one open at a time, close the others when an item reports `Changed()`. Up, Down, Home and End move between headers.

## Collapsible

`components.Collapsible` is one animated disclosure without a box. `ArrowPosition` puts the arrow before or after the label, and `Leading` and `Trailing` add an icon or a count to the header.
