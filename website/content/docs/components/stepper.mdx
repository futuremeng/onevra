---
title: Stepper
description: Shows where a person is in a multi-step flow, such as checkout or setup.
---

<Example name="stepper" height={220} />

`current` is the index of the step in progress; set it to the number of steps once all are done. Completed steps show a check and failed steps an alert. With `Selectable`, people can return to completed steps, which `Chosen()` reports. `Orientation: components.Vertical` stacks the steps for a sidebar, and `Compact` shows markers only.
