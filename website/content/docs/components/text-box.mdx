---
title: TextBox
description: A text field, single-line or multiline, with optional icons and prefixes.
---

<Example name="text-box" height={260} />

`components.TextBox` edits a `*string`. Chain MyGo's input methods on it, such as `Label`, `Placeholder` and `Password`. `LeadingIcon` and `LeadingText` add a decoration or a prefix that is not part of the value, and `ReadOnly` keeps the text selectable but not editable.

## Multiline

`Multiline` accepts line breaks. `Lines(min, max)` grows the field with its text and scrolls past the maximum.

<Example name="text-box-multiline" height={240} />

## Buffers

`components.TextBoxBuffer` edits a `*ui.TextBuffer` the application keeps, so editing a long document does not copy it into a string every frame.
