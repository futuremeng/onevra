---
title: PixelGrid
description: A themed grid background for canvases and artboards.
---

<Example name="pixel-grid" height={280} />

PixelGrid draws one-point lines in the theme's colors, with every few lines emphasized. Children draw above it and are clipped to its bounds. It takes no input.
