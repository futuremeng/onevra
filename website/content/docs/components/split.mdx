---
title: Split
description: Two resizable panes with a draggable divider, side by side or stacked.
---

<Example name="split" height={360} />

`components.Split` binds the first pane's size in points; `components.SplitPercent` binds its share, so both panes grow with the window. Dragging the divider or focusing it and pressing the arrow keys resizes the panes, and double-clicking restores `Default`. Minimum sizes keep both panes usable.
