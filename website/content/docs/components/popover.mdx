---
title: Popover
description: A non-modal panel anchored to a control, for options and short forms.
---

<Example name="popover" height={340} />

Build the anchor first, then call `components.Popover` with it every frame. The panel opens below the anchor and flips above it when there is no room. Escape and a press outside close it; the press continues to whatever was under the pointer.

For a hint that is never interactive, use a [tooltip](/docs/components/tooltip).
