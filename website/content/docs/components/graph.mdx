title: Relationship graph
description: A force-directed graph of nodes and edges, panned, zoomed and dragged.
---

<Example name="graph" height={440} />

`graph.View` lays out nodes with forces and keeps the layout and camera in a `graph.State`. Nodes need stable IDs; groups seed their placement and color. Drag nodes, use the wheel or a pinch to zoom, and double-click an openable node to open it. Hovering a node highlights its neighbors.
