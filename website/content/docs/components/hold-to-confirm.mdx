---
title: HoldToConfirm
description: A button that acts only after it is held, for actions a stray click must not trigger.
---

<Example name="hold-to-confirm" height={160} />

Press and hold the button: a fill tracks the hold, and letting go early rewinds it. When the hold completes, the button sets its `*bool` and morphs into its confirmed label. Space and Enter hold it from the keyboard.

Prefer an [alert dialog](/docs/components/dialog) when people need to read the consequences first. Set the value back to `false` to reset the button.
