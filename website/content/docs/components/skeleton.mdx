---
title: Skeleton
description: A pulsing placeholder in the shape of content that is still loading.
---

<Example name="skeleton" height={200} />

`components.SkeletonContent` shows a skeleton while its `loading` flag is set and the content once it clears: the placeholder fades out as the content rises in, and the height springs between them. `components.Skeleton` draws the placeholder alone.
