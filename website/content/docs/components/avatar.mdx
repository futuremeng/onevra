title: Avatar
description: A person or account as a photo or initials, alone or in an overlapping group.
---

<Example name="avatar" height={160} />

Without an image, an avatar shows up to two initials of the name. `Status` adds a presence dot, which assistive technology hears with the name. Decode photos outside builders and pass the bitmap; pass nil while loading or after a failure.

## Groups

`components.AvatarGroup` overlaps members in order and folds those past `Max` into a count. Pointing at the stack loosens it and names the member under the pointer; members joining or leaving open and close their slots.
