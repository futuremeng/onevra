---
title: ScrollEdgeBar
description: A bar of controls floating over the edge of scrolling content, on glass, a soft edge or a blur.
---

<Example name="scroll-edge-bar" height={400} />

Build the bar after the content, inside a bounded container that clips: the bar samples what is painted beneath it. Pad the content by `ScrollEdgeBarExtent` so its first rows and the scroll bar start clear of the bar. Switch the styles above while scrolling to compare them.

`ScrollEdgeGlass` sets the whole bar on Liquid Glass. The other styles leave the bar transparent, so its controls bring their own faces, such as `IconButtonToolbar`. `components.ScrollEdge` is the effect alone, for hosts that place their own controls.
