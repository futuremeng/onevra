---
title: Switch
description: Turns a setting on and off at once, with a knob that lifts into glass while held.
---

<Example name="switch" height={180} />

A tap toggles the switch; holding or dragging the knob lifts it as a glass lens that follows the pointer along the track. Space and Enter toggle it from the keyboard.

## Compact

`Compact` draws a smaller switch for dense rows such as table cells and toolbars. `HideLabel` hides the text while keeping it as the accessible name.
