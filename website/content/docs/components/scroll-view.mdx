---
title: ScrollView
description: A scrolling container, vertical, horizontal or both, with MyGo's scroll bars.
---

<Example name="scroll-view" height={260} />

Give a scroll view a size, or let it fill a bounded parent. Vertical and two-way views arrange children in a column, and horizontal views in a row. `ScrollbarInsets` moves the scroll bar track independently of the content's padding, which helps when a bar floats over an edge.

Let a scroll view reach the edges of the surface it scrolls within, and pad its content instead: an inset view clips scroll bars and gestures before they reach the edge.
