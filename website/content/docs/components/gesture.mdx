---
title: Gestures
description: Pan and zoom for custom canvases, from wheels, touchpads and pinches.
---

<Example name="gesture" height={360} />

`gesture.Navigation` turns an element's scrolling into pan and zoom events: a touchpad pans and a pinch zooms, and a mouse wheel zooms. Keep one navigation per element and apply the events to your own camera. Graphs and flow diagrams use it for theirs.
